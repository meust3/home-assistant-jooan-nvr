# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and privacy-conscious. Accept feedback
gracefully, focus criticism on the work, and avoid publishing another person's
private device, account, network, or location information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, trolling,
and publication of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security
reporting channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy where possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
